ÉCOUTER UNE SCÈNE — SCRIPTS À ADAPTER
Environ 50–65 secondes par scène à un débit posé. Les noms et histoires sont fictifs.
Les audios ne sont pas encore enregistrés.

1. Où sont mes lunettes ?
Where are my glasses?

Tom is in the kitchen, looking for his glasses. He is on his hands and knees, looking under the wooden table. There are plates, a phone, a mug and some bread on the table, but his glasses aren't there.

His daughter can see them. She's pointing at his head and smiling. He's been wearing them on top of his head all this time!

Meanwhile, their cat has climbed into a shopping bag beside the table. It looks very comfortable. Tom isn't the only one who has found an unusual place to put something. Can you spot the glasses before he does?

2. Un pique-nique mouvementé
A windy picnic

Anna and Ben have brought their lunch to the park. There's a red blanket on the grass, with a basket, two apples, a bottle and two cups. Anna is sitting on the blanket, holding a sandwich.

It looks like a lovely day for a picnic, except for one thing: the wind. Ben's hat has blown off his head, and he's trying to catch it before it gets away.

While they're looking at the hat, their dog is running off with a bread roll in its mouth. If they don't look down soon, there won't be much lunch left for them!

3. La chaussette disparue
The missing sock

Lucy is sorting the washing in the living room. She's holding a red sock, but she can't find the other one. There's a basket full of clothes on the floor in front of her.

A blue shirt is hanging over a chair, and a pair of shoes is underneath it. On the little table, there's a book beside a mug. Lucy has looked around the room, but she still hasn't found the missing sock.

Perhaps she should ask the cat. It's lying on the sofa, with its paws on something red. Apparently, a clean sock makes a very comfortable cushion.

4. Un passager surprise
An unexpected passenger

Emma is getting ready for a holiday. Her suitcase is open on the bed, and she's already packed some clothes. She's holding a sun hat and wondering where to put it.

Her sunglasses and a book are still on the bed. There's a backpack on the floor, beside a pair of shoes, and a lamp on the bedside table.

But something is taking up a lot of space in her suitcase. Her dog has climbed inside and is sitting among the clothes. It seems to be saying, “Don't forget me!” Emma will have to move her unexpected passenger before she can finish packing.

5. Un café sous la pluie
A rainy afternoon

It's raining, but the cafe is still a pleasant place to spend the afternoon. A man is sitting outside under the awning, reading a newspaper. There's a cup and a plate on his table, and a bicycle is leaning against the wall nearby.

A woman in a yellow coat has stopped beside him. She's holding a red umbrella and wearing yellow boots. She looks quite dry, but her little dog is standing in a puddle.

The man has a spare chair at his table. Perhaps she'll sit down for a coffee while they wait for the rain to stop.

6. Une drôle de jardinière
A very unusual flower pot

A father and his daughter are working in the garden. He's kneeling on the grass with a small trowel in his hand. She's holding a green watering can and carefully watering a flower.

But this flower isn't growing in an ordinary pot. It's growing in an old brown boot! Instead of throwing the boot away, they've found another use for it.

There are two flower pots in the wheelbarrow behind them. A pair of gardening gloves is lying on a wooden stool, and a red ball is on the grass nearby. When they've finished watering, perhaps they'll have time for a game.
